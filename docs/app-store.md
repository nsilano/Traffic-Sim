## Description (4000)

You run the lights. Everyone else just wants to get home.

Gridlock drops you above a busy city intersection with a set of signal buttons and a lot of impatient drivers. Tap a light to turn it green, tap again to send it through yellow to red. Keep traffic flowing, keep the box clear, and never give two crossing streams a green at once.

EVERY DRIVER IS DIFFERENT
Commuters, speeders, cautious drivers and slow trucks all decide for themselves whether to run a yellow. Some pull away late, some stall, some tailgate. Drivers turning right will go on red when it's clear. Read the traffic, not just the lights.

PEOPLE ON FOOT
Crosswalks get busy. Give pedestrians a walk signal when the cars they cross are stopped, and watch for turning drivers. Each walk button shows which way its people cross.

PATIENCE IS THE CLOCK
The driver at the front of each line keeps a timer, shown right beside their button. Leave them waiting too long and the city's patience drains. Run out and the shift is over.

EMERGENCIES AND RUSH HOUR
Ambulances, fire engines and police need a clear path. Rush-hour waves pile cars up fast. Night shifts light the streets with headlights and lamp glow.

SEVEN SHIFTS, THEN FREEPLAY
Work your way from a quiet two-lane crossing to Downtown Rush, with turn lanes, one-way streets, a harbor merge and split phases along the way. Earn up to three stars on each. Then open any map in Freeplay, set the density from Quiet to Gridlock, pick a traffic flow and chase your best score and cars per minute.

BUILT FOR PHONES AND TABLETS
Pixel-art city, chiptune sound, haptics you can feel on every switch. Big buttons, colour-blind signal shapes and other options in Settings. Game Center leaderboards and achievements.

No ads. No in-app purchases. No account. Plays fully offline.
